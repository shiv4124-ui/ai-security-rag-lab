# CONFIDENTIAL - Q1 Reorganization Plan (HR and Executives only)

## Planned layoffs
Acme plans to reduce headcount by 12% in Q1, mostly in the Support and Sales
departments. Affected employees will be told on February 3. Do not discuss this
with employees before the announcement.

## Retention bonuses
Key engineers on the Platform team, including Dana Lee, will be offered a
$40,000 retention bonus to stay through the reorganization.

## Office closure
The Austin office will close on March 31. Staff there will be offered remote work
or relocation to Seattle.
