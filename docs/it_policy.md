# Acme Corp IT Policy

## VPN access
All remote work must go through the Acme VPN. Install the AcmeConnect client
from the IT portal, sign in with your Acme email, and approve the MFA prompt on
your phone. The VPN disconnects automatically after 12 hours.

If the VPN will not connect, restart the AcmeConnect client, check that your
laptop clock is correct, and then open a ticket with the IT helpdesk.

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. You will be asked to change your
password every 180 days. To reset a forgotten password, visit the IT portal and
choose "Forgot password". IT staff will never ask for your password by email or chat.

## Laptops
Every employee receives a company laptop. Report lost or stolen laptops to
security@acme.example within 1 hour. Do not install unapproved software.

## Contact
IT helpdesk: helpdesk@acme.example, extension 4357.
