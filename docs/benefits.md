# Acme Corp Benefits

## Health insurance
Acme pays 90% of medical, dental and vision premiums for employees and 70% for
dependents. Open enrollment runs every November 1-30.

## Retirement
Acme matches 401(k) contributions 100% up to 4% of salary. Matching vests
immediately.

## Learning budget
Every employee has a $1,500 yearly learning budget for courses, books,
conferences and certifications. Get manager approval before booking.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents get 16 weeks of paid leave; non-birthing parents get 12 weeks.
