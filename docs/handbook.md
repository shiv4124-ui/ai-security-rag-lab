# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Paid time off (PTO)
Full-time employees get 20 days of PTO per year, plus 10 company holidays.
PTO accrues monthly. Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next year.
Request PTO in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for trips longer than 3 days.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 sick days per year. You do not need a doctor's note for
absences of 3 days or fewer.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements need approval from your department head and HR.

## Expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days through the finance portal. Meals while
travelling are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Keep all receipts.

## Code of conduct
Treat colleagues with respect. Report concerns to hr@acme.example or the
anonymous ethics line.
