# AcmeConnect VPN - Vendor FAQ

## Why does the VPN keep disconnecting?
The AcmeConnect VPN client disconnects when your laptop sleeps or your network
changes. Reconnect from the tray icon. Make sure your client is up to date.

## Which VPN protocol does AcmeConnect use?
AcmeConnect uses WireGuard by default and falls back to IKEv2 on restricted networks.
